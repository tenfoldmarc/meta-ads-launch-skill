# Countries

## Default: the Big 5

`US, CA, GB, AU, NZ` (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand). English-speaking, high purchasing power, the usual starting point.

## Tier 1 add-ons

Offer these during onboarding. English-language ads do best where English is widely spoken, so the list is ordered by that.

| Group | Countries (codes) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| English-first | Ireland `IE` | Same language, same buyer. Easiest add. |
| High English proficiency | Netherlands `NL`, Sweden `SE`, Norway `NO`, Denmark `DK`, Finland `FI` | English ads usually work fine. |
| Wealthy, mixed English | Germany `DE`, Switzerland `CH`, Austria `AT`, Belgium `BE`, Luxembourg `LU` | Works, often better with translated ads. |
| Big Western Europe | France `FR`, Spain `ES`, Italy `IT` | Lower English response. Best with translated creatives and landing page. |
| Other high-income | Singapore `SG`, United Arab Emirates `AE`, Israel `IL`, Japan `JP` | Test separately; behavior differs a lot. |

## Heads-up worth telling the user once

When one ad set targets many countries, Meta spends where conversions are cheapest, which is often not the country you care about most. If the US matters most, or the mix includes non-English countries, watch the country breakdown in the first reviews. If one country eats budget at a bad CPA, the fix is a separate ad set or campaign for it, not a bigger list.
